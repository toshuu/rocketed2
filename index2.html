🎓 Rocketed · Interactive Learning

A lesson becomes a guided experience that the learner explores at their own pace.

What makes the experience
💬

Conversation-led

Short prompts and clickable answers guide learners from one part of the lesson to the next.

📖

Story-based lessons

Information is arranged as a beginning, chapters, and an ending to build a clear narrative.

🖼️

Visual storytelling

Images, animated transitions, and slides make each step more engaging and easier to follow.

🎯

Interest selection

Learners choose subjects such as technology, business, science, history, and art.

🧭

Guided progression

Each answer can lead to a different next step, so the learner actively moves through the material.

🧩

Reusable lesson format

The same interactive structure can present different topics and educational stories.

How a learner uses it

1Choose a topicPick an area of interest
2Start a storyMeet the subject and context
3Explore chaptersRead, see, and discover
4Make choicesTap answers to continue
5Reach the takeawayFinish the guided lesson
Core idea: learning by exploration
